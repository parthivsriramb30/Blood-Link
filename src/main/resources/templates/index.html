Core Business Rule: 90-Day Cooldown Period

Following medical safety guidelines, a blood donor must not donate blood more than once every 90 days. When a donation is recorded in BloodLink, the service layer marks the donor as COOLDOWN and automatically hides them from search results. After the exact 90 days elapse (currentDate >= lastDonationDate + 90), the donor automatically becomes available again without manual database changes.
